We tend to treat readiness as a prerequisite — a state we'll eventually reach, after which the work becomes possible. In practice, it rarely arrives on schedule.

## Clarity follows action

You don't think your way into readiness so much as act your way into it. The first imperfect step generates the information the next one needs. Waiting for certainty just delays the learning.

## Small, honest beginnings

The goal isn't a dramatic leap. It's a start small enough that you can't talk yourself out of it, and honest enough that it moves you somewhere real. Momentum is built, not found.

If you've been waiting to feel ready, consider that the feeling may be on the other side of starting — not before it.
