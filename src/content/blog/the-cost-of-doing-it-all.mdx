There's a particular kind of person who never drops the ball — and pays for it quietly. When you're the reliable one, the capable one, the work keeps arriving because everyone knows it will get done.

## Competence is not the same as capacity

Being able to do something isn't the same as having room for it. The most capable people are often the most overextended, precisely because their competence hides the cost until something gives.

## Choosing the work that matters

The shift isn't doing more, faster. It's getting deliberate about what deserves your attention — and letting the rest go without guilt. That's not a productivity hack. It's a different relationship with your own time.

Start by naming the one thing that would make the biggest difference this month. Then protect it like it matters — because it does.
